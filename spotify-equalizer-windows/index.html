Equalizer

A Spotify equalizer, Winamp style.

Spotiamp+ gives your music Winamp's 10-band equalizer, with a preamp, presets and a curve for each song or artist. It works on everything Spotiamp+ plays itself: Spotify with Premium, your own files and the radio.

Download Spotiamp+ See everything it does

Free · open source · Windows 10 and 11 · not affiliated with Spotify or Winamp

Ten bands and a preamp

From 60 Hz to 16 kHz, 12 dB up or down each, the bands Winamp had. Drag a slider and you hear it straight away.

Presets, Winamp's own too

Pick a preset, save your own, or load Winamp's .eqf files. Share your favourite curve with the community on Discord.

AUTO: a curve for each song

Save a curve for a song or a whole artist, switch AUTO on, and it comes back by itself whenever they play.

Even loudness

Normalize volume evens out songs that are mastered loud or quiet, so you don't reach for the volume between them.

Boost your music.
