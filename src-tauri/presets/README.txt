MilkDrop presets that come with Spotiamp+.
Each one is by the author named at the start of its file name. Classic MilkDrop
presets were released freely, without a specific licence; if you made one of
these and want it out, open an issue at github.com/fdeox/spotiamp-plus.
Your own presets go in %APPDATA%\spotiamp\config\milkdrop (right-click the
MilkDrop picture: Open the presets folder).
