Textures the bundled MilkDrop presets draw with (clouds, lichen, fire, the
ones "random texture" presets pick from).

Most are projectM's MilkDrop texture pack, the textures released with MilkDrop
and others presets use widely:
  https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/presets-milkdrop-texture-pack
The rest (bee*, chloemall*, lavatex, TheSunTex) come from BeatDrop's, for the
presets by Incubo_ (Se7enSlasher) that ask for them by name:
  https://github.com/OfficialIncubo/BeatDrop-Music-Visualizer
Large ones were scaled down to at most 1024 px.

Your own textures go in %APPDATA%\spotiamp\config\milkdrop (any folder in it).
