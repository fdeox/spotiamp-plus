The visualizer that made Winamp famous, moving to your Spotify music. Spotiamp+ draws real MilkDrop presets with projectM, the open source MilkDrop. Free, for Windows 10 and 11.

Free · open source · Windows 10 and 11 · not affiliated with Spotify or Winamp

534 presets, ready to go

Picked from the Cream of the Crop collection to run smoothly, plus 183 by Incubo, among them his takes on Spotiamp+'s own patterns. Press M in the visualizer and they move with every beat.

Make it yours

  • L opens the preset list: search it, heart your favourites and change only between them.
  • Change presets every 15 seconds to 2 minutes, or cut on the beat. Pin one to keep it.
  • Drop your own .milk files and textures in its folder.

Full screen

Double-click the visualizer for full screen, Esc to come back. The song's title flashes up when it changes.

What it needs

Graphics with OpenGL 3.3, which nearly every PC from the last ten years has. Presets made for MilkDrop 3 (.milk2) may not load, and BeatDrop's spectrum and waveform functions work.
